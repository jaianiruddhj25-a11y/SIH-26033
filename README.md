## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1. Detailed Explanation:
Develop an AI-powered digital marketplace connecting farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers. It provides product listings, transparent pricing, online payments, and delivery support.

2. How It Addresses the Problem:
It reduces intermediaries, helps farmers earn better profits, and allows consumers to purchase agricultural products at reasonable prices.

3. Innovation and Uniqueness:
AI-based demand forecasting, price analysis, smart delivery routes, and regional-language support make the platform efficient and farmer-friendly.

4. Smart Price Recommendation

The platform will analyze available market prices, seasonal trends, and product demand to suggest suitable selling prices for farmers. This helps farmers make informed selling decisions and improves price transparency for consumers.

5. Direct Order and Supply Management

The platform will allow farmers to manage product availability, receive orders, and track sales in one place. Buyers can check available quantities and place orders directly, improving coordination and reducing unnecessary delays.

## Technical Approach

1. Technologies Used:

- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js
- Backend: Python and Django
- Database: PostgreSQL or MySQL
- AI/ML: Scikit-learn and Pandas
- Logistics: Mapping and routing APIs

2. Implementation Methodology:

1. Register farmers and buyers.
2. Allow farmers to list products and prices.
3. Enable buyers to search and place orders.
4. Use AI to predict demand and analyze prices.
5. Manage delivery, payments, and order tracking.

3. AI-Based Demand Forecasting

Machine-learning algorithms will analyze historical sales, seasonal patterns, and available market data to estimate future demand for agricultural products. These predictions will help farmers plan their sales and reduce the risk of unsold produce.

4.  System Integration and Data Management

The frontend, backend, and database will work together to manage farmer profiles, product listings, orders, and transactions. Django APIs will facilitate communication between the frontend and backend, while the database stores and retrieves information efficiently.

5. Security and Performance

The platform will use secure authentication, role-based access, input validation, and appropriate data protection measures. Testing will be performed to verify functionality, prevent common errors, and ensure reliable performance as the number of users increases.   
   
## Feasibility and Viability

1. Feasibility Analysis:
The project is technically feasible using existing technologies. Open-source tools reduce development costs, while a simple mobile-friendly interface makes it practical for farmers.

2. Challenges and Risks:

- Limited digital literacy among farmers.
- Transportation and storage difficulties.
- Agricultural price fluctuations.
- Payment security and data privacy concerns.

3. Strategies to Overcome Challenges:
Provide regional-language support, partner with local transport providers, update market prices regularly, and implement secure payment systems.

## Impact and Benefits
1. Impact on Target Audience:

- Farmers can reach more buyers and improve earnings.
- Consumers can purchase products at competitive prices.
- Retailers can source products directly.
- Rural communities benefit from improved digital access.

2. Benefits of the Solution:

Social: Supports farmers and improves market accessibility.

Economic: Reduces unnecessary costs and improves price transparency.

Environmental: Optimized delivery routes and better demand forecasting can reduce fuel consumption and food wastage.
